Learner Rights and Protection

A brief introduction to important protections. Consult the full official issuances for exact requirements and procedures.

🧒

Right to Protection

Children must be protected from abuse, exploitation, violence, neglect, and other forms of harm.

RA 7610 • DepEd Order 40, s. 2012
🚫

Protection from Bullying

Schools have responsibilities to prevent and address bullying, including cyberbullying, under applicable rules.

RA 10627 • DepEd Order 55, s. 2013
🏫

Safe Learning Environment

Learning environments should support safety, dignity, inclusion, and learner well-being.

Check current DepEd issuances
⚖️

Fair and Respectful Treatment

Learners should be treated with dignity and without unjust discrimination.

🗣️

Right to Be Heard

Learners should have appropriate opportunities to express their views in matters affecting them.

🤲

Right to Seek Help

Learners should be able to approach trusted adults and appropriate protection channels.

Where to Get Help

If you feel unsafe or are worried about another learner, contact a trusted adult.

School Head

Provides school leadership and helps ensure appropriate school response.

Guidance Designate / Counselor

May provide guidance support and help connect learners to appropriate services.

School LRP / Child Protection Personnel

Receives concerns through authorized school procedures and coordinates appropriate action.

Trusted Adult

A parent, guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult who can help you reach support.

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or appropriate child-protection authorities. Do not rely on this website for emergency response.
